Option C: Wallpaper first

Each theme fills the screen like a poster, and the controls sit on a dark panel underneath. You browse themes by looking at them, not by reading a list.

Good for: Best once you have several themes to flick through. With only one theme it can feel a bit empty.

The same three screens appear in all three options, so you can compare like with like. The app stays small in every option, at roughly 200 to 300 KB.
